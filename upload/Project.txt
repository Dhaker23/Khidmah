I want you to design and build a production-quality freelance marketplace specifically designed for **Tunisian freelancers and Tunisian/international clients**.

The platform should be inspired by the strongest concepts of:

* Upwork
* Fiverr
* 5amsat
* Other professional freelance marketplaces

But DO NOT clone them.

The platform must be designed around a major problem:

> Tunisian freelancers have skills and talent, but often face difficulties finding trustworthy clients, proving their professionalism, receiving payments, and withdrawing earnings through methods that work for them.

The platform should solve this through:

**Identity + Professional Verification + Portfolio + Contracts + Payment Protection + Reviews + Secure Transactions + Local/International Withdrawal Architecture**

# 1. CORE PRODUCT IDEA

The platform is:

> A trusted freelance marketplace connecting verified Tunisian talent with clients in Tunisia and around the world.

The platform should provide two sides:

## Freelancer

A Tunisian freelancer creates an account, builds a professional profile, submits verification information and portfolio work, and waits for admin approval.

After approval, the freelancer can:

* Apply to jobs
* Create services
* Communicate with clients
* Create contracts
* Complete projects
* Receive earnings
* Request withdrawals
* Build reputation

## Client

A client can:

* Create an account
* Post jobs

* View portfolios
* Compare profiles
* Invite freelancers
* Create contracts
* Fund milestones
* Review work
* Leave reviews

# 19. ADMIN DECISION

Admin has three primary choices:

## APPROVE

Freelancer becomes:

VERIFIED FREELANCER

## REJECT

Freelancer cannot operate as a marketplace freelancer.

"Your application needs additional information."

"Please provide a clearer portfolio example."

"Please update your professional biography."

"Please provide additional verification information."

"Please replace the current profile photo."

# 22. IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION ABOUT "REFUND"

Do NOT automatically "refund" an applicant merely because their freelancer application is rejected.

Account registration should normally be free.

Therefore:

If freelancer registration/application is free:

There is nothing to refund.

If you later introduce a paid verification service or paid feature:

The refund rules must be explicitly defined.

For the MVP:

FREELANCER VERIFICATION = FREE

REJECTION = NO PAYMENT / NO REFUND REQUIRED

This keeps the trust system simple and fair.
